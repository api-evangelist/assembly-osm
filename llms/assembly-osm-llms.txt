# Assembly OSM

> Assembly OSM is a New York City off-site manufacturing company founded in 2019 by SHoP Architects co-founders Bill and Chris Sharples. It industrialises high-rise urban housing with a "post-modular" kit-of-parts chassis fabricated across a distributed supplier network and stacked on site, targeting market-competitive high-rise residential delivery in roughly 60% of conventional schedule. Assembly OSM sells buildings, not software: it publishes no developer program, no public API, no SDK, and no machine-readable API contract.
